# LanderViz Rev A — fabrication requirements

2026-11-06. **For confirmation; not production approval.** These notes replace
prior fabrication notes. Copper/drill geometry remains `marking-20261105`.

- Finished board 48 × 42 mm, four layers, nominal 1.6 mm, FR-4, blue solder
  mask, white silkscreen, lead-free HASL (hot-air solder levelling) finish.
  Confirm colour/finish in the final order summary before production.
- Preserve outline/notch and connector access. Include handling rails and
  removal as needed; deliver individual boards. Fabrication electrical test
  required. Inner copper order: In1_Cu then In2_Cu.
- Reference stack, top to bottom: 35 µm copper / 0.0994 mm dielectric
  (3313, relative permittivity 4.1) / 15.2 µm ground copper / 1.265 mm core
  (relative permittivity 4.6) / 15.2 µm inner copper / 0.0994 mm dielectric /
  35 µm copper. Return proposed stack for approval. No impedance certification
  requested; equal total thickness alone does not establish equivalent stack.
- Minimum drill 0.20 mm; J3 plated slots 0.40 mm wide. Confirm capability.
- U1/U2 ground-pad holes remain unfilled; coordinate acceptance with assembly.
  No hole enlargement, filling/capping or design changes without approval.
- Preserve supplied J1/J2 chamfered outer ground lands and locating holes.

Confirm these requirements and disclose exceptions/extra charges in the final
manufacturing summary before customer authorization.
